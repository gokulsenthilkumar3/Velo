# ev-share product profile

## Name

**ev-share**

## Short description

An EV scooter-sharing platform for convenient, affordable local travel in India’s Tier-2 cities.

## Positioning

ev-share makes short urban trips dependable without requiring scooter ownership. Riders locate, reserve, unlock, and pay for nearby electric scooters from an Android app. Local operators get real-time fleet visibility, practical service workflows, and data that improves availability and vehicle health.

## Target market

The initial market is India’s Tier-2 cities: dense local corridors where public transport and private vehicle ownership do not always meet short-distance travel needs. The service is designed for commuters, students, workers, and visitors making first- and last-mile or intra-city trips.

## Value proposition

| Audience | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Riders | A nearby, transparent and cashless short-trip option. |
| Operators | A single control plane for vehicles, rides, pricing, service work, and performance. |
| Cities and partners | Lower-emission local mobility with auditable operating data. |

## Product principles

- **Availability before novelty:** riders must be able to find a safe, charged, rideable scooter.
- **Local operations matter:** workflows support field teams, not only dashboards.
- **Trust is designed in:** prices, ride status, payments, and support outcomes are clear.
- **Data should reduce downtime:** telemetry informs maintenance before faults interrupt service.
